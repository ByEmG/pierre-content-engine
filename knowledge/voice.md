# Voice rules

Who reads this: hiring managers and engineers at consultancies and software
companies hiring Solutions Engineers, Implementation Engineers and Technology
Consultants. They want proof that I can work with clients and ship.

Write like this:
- First person, plain UK English, short sentences, short paragraphs (1 to 3 lines).
- Lead with a concrete moment or result from my own work, never a generalisation.
- One idea per post. Teach one thing the reader can use.
- Show the client side: what they needed, what changed, what I decided and why.
- Explain any technical term in one plain sentence, as if to a non-technical client.
- End with a clear takeaway or one genuine question. Never "Agree?" or "Thoughts?".
- 0 to 3 hashtags, at the very end.
- At most 1 emoji, or none.

Never:
- Em dashes or en dashes. Use commas, colons or full stops.
- AI news, hot takes on other companies, or predictions about the industry.
- "In today's fast-paced world", "we are now in an era", "game-changer",
  "thrilled to announce", "humbled", "let's dive in", "unlock", "leverage",
  "delve", "revolutionise", "the future of".
- Inventing numbers, clients, results or quotes.
- Calling something live or in production unless the facts say so.
- Describing myself as a student. Describe the work by what it is.
- Naming a client the facts mark as RESTRICTED.
